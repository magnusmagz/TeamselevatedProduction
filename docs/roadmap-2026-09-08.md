next and in what order, and what is parked with the trigger that would unpark it. Supersedes the

## The rules (unchanged)
One branch, one revert. Failing test first. Additive migrations with reverse SQL, applied through
`scripts/apply-migration.php`. A switch on anything that sends. Frontend first when auth tightens,
backend first when a page calls a new endpoint. Smoke test after every deploy.

## Horizon 2 — Next (rest of September into October)

### 2.1 GOTR G0 with GOTR, then their first council
Discovery is still owed: the requirement matrix per role, validity periods, whether the council
reviews uploads or accepts them, what national staff edit, and their current coach export with
council codes. Engineering that follows discovery, all small because the model exists:
- seed the tree (national → divisions → councils) and the baseline requirements from the matrix
- import their export through the national importer (dark today) on a five-row test first
- the GOTR later-later rows only if they ask: email-in submissions, local training search,
  contact-your-council-leader, SSO. Each is a week or less; none is scheduled.

### 2.2 Referee management for tournaments (2C) — only if the rehearsal says so
Assignment UI over the tables that already exist, availability, conflict detection (child on team),
confirmation. About two weeks. The single biggest operational bottleneck in youth tournaments
according to the gap analysis, and the single largest unbuilt piece, so it waits for evidence from
the rehearsal rather than an assumption.

### 2.3 Tournament finances and eligibility (3A, 3B) — same trigger
Discount codes (already punted for clubs; may return for tournaments), invoice PDF, roster freeze,
waiver e-signature (the signing table from decision 15 serves both compliance and this). Two to three
weeks combined.

### 2.4 Spanish for outbound comms (L1–L4)
Scoped and slotted: language preference per person, Spanish template variants with English
fallback, `language_sent` on the log. About a week. Trigger: CKU's Spanish-speaking families ask, or
a second club with them does. Portal strings (408) are already translated for when the portal
follows.

### 2.5 Payments 5 — ACH, saved methods, autopay
Trigger: a club with installment plans asks, or GOTR councils need dues. Two to three weeks, each
piece behind its own switch per the payments dossier.
