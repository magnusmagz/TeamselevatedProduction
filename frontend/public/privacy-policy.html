Privacy Policy

Teams Elevated – Sports Club Management
Effective Date: March 17, 2026
Developed by 4Msquared LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how Teams Elevated ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application and website at https://teams-elevated.netlify.app (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide the Service:

  • Account information: name, email address, and password
  • Profile information: role within a sports club (admin, coach, parent)
  • Athlete information: name, date of birth, gender, team assignments, and attendance records
  • Payment records: payment status and history (we do not store full payment card details)
  • Communications: messages sent through the in-app chat feature
  • Usage data: log data and device information for security and performance purposes

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Teams Elevated is used by sports clubs that manage youth athletes. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. Athlete data is entered by a parent, guardian, or authorized club administrator on behalf of the child.
  • Parents and guardians have the right to review, correct, or request deletion of their child's personal information at any time.
  • To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@4msquared.com.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Provide and operate the Teams Elevated platform
  • Manage team rosters, schedules, and communications
  • Process and display payment status information
  • Respond to support requests
  • Ensure the security and integrity of the Service

4. We Do Not Sell or Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information or athlete data with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Data is never used for advertising.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data at rest
  • Encrypted transmission via HTTPS/TLS
  • Access controls limiting data to authorized users within their club

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@4msquared.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Upon a deletion request, we will remove your data within 30 days except where retention is required by law.

8. Third-Party Services

The Service may use the following third-party services:

  • Netlify – web hosting
  • Heroku – backend infrastructure
  • Google Maps – location display features

These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
