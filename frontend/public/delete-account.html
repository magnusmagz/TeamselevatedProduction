Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Teams Elevated account and all associated data at any time.

Option 1 — In-App (Parents)

If you are a parent or guardian, you can delete your account directly in the app:

  1. Open the Teams Elevated app
  2. Tap More in the bottom navigation
  3. Tap Account Settings
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Option 2 — Email Request (All Users)

Any user — including coaches and admins — can request account deletion by emailing us:

Email Us to Delete Your Account

Please include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request and delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your user account and login credentials
  • Your profile information
  • Any messages you have sent
  • Your association with any clubs or teams

Note: Athlete records added by a club administrator are managed by the club and are not deleted as part of a user account deletion request. Contact your club administrator to request athlete record removal.
