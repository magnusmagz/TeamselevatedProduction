Compliance & Safety Checklist

Coach Requirements

  • Background check (annual)
  • CPR certification
  • First aid training
  • Abuse prevention training
  • Concussion protocol training

Player Requirements

  • Signed liability waiver
  • Medical authorization
  • Emergency contacts on file
  • Age verification (birth cert)
  • Insurance info on file

Organization Requirements

  • Liability insurance active
  • Waivers stored securely (7+ yrs)
  • Incident reporting process
  • Code of conduct published
  • Weather/rainout policy
